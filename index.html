The debrief problem

Good interviews create too much to remember.

Every interviewer leaves with a different version of the conversation. The useful evidence is there — but scattered across notes, memory, and opinion.

01 / CONTEXT FADES

Important moments get reduced to a feeling.

By the time the team meets, the detail behind an interviewer’s impression is often gone.

02 / SIGNALS VARY

Every interviewer listens for something different.

Without a shared rubric, teams unintentionally compare different versions of “good.”

03 / DECISIONS DRAG

Debriefs become a search for evidence.

Hiring teams spend the meeting reconstructing the interview instead of making a decision.
